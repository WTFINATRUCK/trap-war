## Reference Signals

1. A close, grounded over-the-shoulder Hero composition.
2. Warm low-key practical lighting with controlled window contrast.
3. A compact, dense, lived-in safehouse: bed, layered textiles, nightstand, lamp, window/blinds, radiator, TV, desk, books, shoes, posters, and planning material.
4. Dark timber, aged plaster, charcoal, deep green, warm brass, and restrained cool screen accents.
5. A quiet top economy bar with cash, day, and actions.
6. A two-line objective panel that explains intent and names the immediate control.
7. Intentional location and interaction framing rather than development labels.
8. Trap Phone presentation that remains readable without covering the Hero or room hierarchy.

## M8.4 Implementation Boundary

The rebuild must use real runtime geometry, lighting, characters, UI, and interactions. The reference image must never be placed behind gameplay to simulate fidelity. Existing zero-budget character proportions, generic animation, modular environment assets, and absent facial blend shapes remain known constraints until replacement art is authorized.

## Initial Reference-Match Changes

- Reframed exploration camera closer to the Hero with a lower pitch and narrower field of view.
- Reworked safehouse floor, plaster, bed textile, rug, and practical-light palette.
- Added ceiling enclosure, window depth, city silhouettes, blinds, radiator, bedside furniture/light, TV console, posters, plan board, desk chair, drawers, and warm window key.
- Expanded the objective panel with contextual intent and a visible phone-control cue.
- Moved interaction prompts to a restrained lower-right treatment and added a lower-left safehouse location plate.

## Honest Target Gap

The image establishes the product direction, not an immediate asset-quality claim. Current runtime improvements can match composition, hierarchy, lighting intent, density, palette, and UI behavior. Photoreal character anatomy, cloth, hair, facial performance, bespoke furniture, high-resolution materials, and a detailed city exterior remain outside the present zero-budget source-art ceiling.
