## Classification Rule

- **TEMPORARY:** evaluation material, development placeholder, or proof asset.
- **PRODUCTION CANDIDATE:** legally usable and technically plausible, pending file-level and art-direction approval.
- **FINAL-QUALITY:** approved by art, narrative/authenticity, technical art, and production after runtime acceptance.

No M8.2B asset is classified FINAL-QUALITY. The retained Quaternius/Kenney assets remain legally usable CC0 production candidates but continue to set the visible art ceiling.
