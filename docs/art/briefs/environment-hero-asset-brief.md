# Trap Wars - Environment Hero Asset Brief

## Scope

P1 begins only after Hero/Ray visual level is approved. Upgrade assets visible repeatedly in Day 1 screenshots/video; do not design an entire city or alter the existing footprint, route, colliders, or gameplay.

## Safehouse Needs

- One believable focal bed and textile set.
- Desk/chair group with phone charging detail and player-identity props.
- Wardrobe/storage group, door/window treatment, and one authored wall/surface family.
- Eight to twelve restrained identity props with clear clutter logic.
- 1K-2K shared atlases/trim materials for painted wall, wood, textile, plastic, metal, and glass.

## Street/Hub Needs

- Two hero storefront facade treatments and one memorable original Hub sign/counter kit.
- Road/sidewalk/curb hero surfaces and patch/decal set.
- Two believable parked vehicle variants visible in primary frames.
- Ray-area dressing, utility cluster, bench/lighting/signage, and selected street props.
- Shared 1K-2K atlases/trim sheets; no unauthorized brands or real artist work.

## Delivery And Acceptance

Provide FBX/runtime exports, agreed sources, pivots, dimensions, UVs, LODs, colliders where needed, URP maps/material guide, texture budgets, dependencies, and license/rights record. Assets must preserve current modular dimensions and reach 4/5 in the existing Hero, Ray, Hub, safehouse, street, Day, and Night cameras.
