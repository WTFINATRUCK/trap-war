# M8 Art Direction

## Identity

Trap Wars combines premium fintech discipline, hip-hop editorial confidence, and grounded contemporary street culture. The base is precise and trustworthy; street texture is a controlled overlay. The project avoids generic cyberpunk, Web3 gradients, token logos, parody, and visual stereotypes.

## Visual principles

- Contemporary, grounded, aspirational, gritty, and readable.
- Ink, paper, money green, signal gold, weathered masonry, asphalt, metal, and practical warm light.
- Clean financial hierarchy with limited distressed accents.
- Original or clearly licensed decals, flyers, signs, and markings only.
- Cultural specificity must express community, daily life, ambition, pressure, humor, and consequence, not gratuitous criminal imagery.
- Night must remain navigable and legible; black levels cannot erase faces, paths, or interaction targets.

## Character direction

The hero needs a confident contemporary streetwear silhouette with restrained accessories and human proportions. Ray must read as an established person in the neighborhood, not a quest marker. Available CC0 models prove the pipeline but are stylized generic production candidates, not final character designs.

## Interface direction

Trap Phone, HUD, and market use disciplined spacing, concise copy, strong numerical hierarchy, restrained confirmation motion, and consistent green/gold consequence language. Coinbase is an influence only in product clarity, never a source to copy.

## Content review

The current block is intentionally free of real brands and recognizable artist work. The generated decal atlas is abstract and project-owned. A professional character/environment art pass still needs an authenticity review with the narrative owner before any asset is classified FINAL-QUALITY.
