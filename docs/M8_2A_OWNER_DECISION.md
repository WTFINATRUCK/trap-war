## Superseding Owner Decision - 2026-08-18

**OWNER_SELECTED_ZERO_BUDGET_PATH**

The owner declined paid procurement and authorized M8.2B as a zero-budget in-house art upgrade. No vendor outreach, account creation, quote request, purchase, commission, or paid asset acquisition was performed. The procurement checklist below is retained only as the historical alternative that was not selected.

## What Do We Need First?

Approve one coherent Hero/Ray procurement strategy, one rights posture, and one paid evaluation milestone. Character identity, face, rig, and the four test motions are the first spend.

## What Can Wait?

Safehouse/street hero assets, full ambience/music, final Ray dialogue, extra wardrobe, crowds, secondary faces, and portrait/PFP production can wait until the two lead characters pass evaluation.

## What Can Be Bought?

A proven human base/tool license, a licensed locomotion foundation, selective ambience/cue libraries, and pipeline tools can be bought after license review. A purchased base is not an approved character by itself.

## What Should Be Custom?

Hero and Ray faces, hair/wardrobe direction, material identity, differentiation, facial expression set, phone motion, Ray dialogue acting, and Ray voice should be custom or heavily art-directed.

## Cheapest Credible Path

**Tier A:** commercial human base plus one strong character artist for two identities/outfits/faces, licensed locomotion, and a small custom motion/voice package. Approve only if the paid Unity evaluation reaches 4/5.

## Best Value Path

**Tier B, recommended:** proven base topology/rig plus a unified custom Hero/Ray art package, facial delivery, licensed locomotion, custom phone/Ray acting, and human Ray voice proof.

## Premium Path

**Tier C:** bespoke concepts, sculpts, topology, textures, hair/clothing, full face, rigs, LODs, motion, and optional voice/audio under a milestone contract.
