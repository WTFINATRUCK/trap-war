# M8 Environment Pipeline

## Modular standard

Runtime exports use meter scale, grounded pivots, predictable local axes, reusable URP materials, and snap modules. The hero block combines wall, window, door, roof, awning, scaffolding, road, curb, prop, vehicle, and vegetation categories without expanding the accepted footprint.

## Hero application

The safehouse adds distinct furniture, technology, cash/economic props, wall art, a modular door, clutter, and warm practical light. The street adds differentiated facades, The Hub identity, awnings, benches, pallets, dumpster, scaffolding, cables, shrubs, parked/ambient vehicles, textured road/concrete, and original atlas decals.

M8.2B adds a bed headboard, pillows/throw, framed cool window, desk lamp, books, shoes, plan label, and a second original editorial print to the safehouse. The street receives facade trim and lit-window rhythm, brighter asphalt response, a dressed Ray corner, a rebuilt counter-based Hub with columns, fascia, planters, pendants and original decals, and hidden gameplay proxy renderers so interaction volumes no longer read as art.

The result is denser and more legible than M8.1, with clearer safehouse/Hub focal compositions and stronger route depth. It still reads as a stylized modular proof rather than final authored neighborhood art. Architecture, vehicles, surface response, and prop silhouette quality remain the main zero-budget ceiling.

## Lighting and future conditions

Day uses a directional key, ambient color, practicals, ACES, and restrained effects. M8.2B slightly raises the directional key and ambient floor, then lifts the Day 2 cool grade so the route and characters remain readable without flattening the practicals. Rain/wetness is not implemented, but reusable materials and centralized atmosphere settings leave room for later weather variants.

## Culling and LOD

The block is compact, so distance/quality activation and `LODGroup` are proportionate. Full open-world streaming is intentionally absent. Representative character and population tiers are implemented; authored building, vehicle, and prop LOD chains remain M8.x production work.
