# Trap Wars V2 - M8.2B Human Playtest Packet

## Build

Run `Builds/M8_2B/TrapWarsM82B.exe` while the local API is available at `http://127.0.0.1:8787`. This is a non-development Windows x64 build. It contains no development overlay, teleport controls, acceptance hotkeys, or debugging player flag.

## Tester Brief

Give no route explanation beyond: "Play through Day 1 until the game tells you Day 2 is waiting. Think aloud when something is confusing or visually unclear." Do not coach the tester toward Ray, the clues, or the market.

Record start time at first control and end time at the Day 2 hook. Capture the full display and audio only with tester consent. Do not use an existing resume record.

## Questions

1. Who felt like the player character, and what details do you remember about them?
2. Who is Ray, and did his body language help the conversation?
3. What did you think the Hub was before opening it?
4. Which environment felt most believable, and which felt most temporary?
5. Did the phone opening or closing feel abrupt?
6. Was any text, route, interaction, or night state hard to read?
7. Where did the experience feel slow, rushed, or confusing?
8. Would this visual quality support an investor demonstration? Why or why not?

## Required Record

Record tester familiarity, hardware, resolution, full duration, route deviations, defects, exact answers, and whether the tester completed without intervention. Do not convert the paced instrumented route into human evidence.
